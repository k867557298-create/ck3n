# Ottoman Empire: Suleiman I

A **Crusader Kings III** mod that adds a compact playable Ottoman scenario at the height of Sultan Suleiman I's rule.

## Included

- The **Suleiman the Magnificent** bookmark on **1 January 1546**.
- Sultan Suleiman I of the Osmanlı dynasty, with ruler traits and skills suited to his historical reputation.
- A green-and-gold **Ottoman Empire** title headquartered in Constantinople, with Anatolia, Thrace, Armenia, Mesopotamia, Jerusalem, and Egypt as constituent kingdoms.
- English and Ukrainian localization.
- A decision for Turkish Ash'ari rulers who control Anatolia and Thrace to establish the Ottoman Empire in another campaign.
- A short opening briefing when beginning on the Suleiman bookmark.

## Install

1. Copy both `descriptor.mod` and the `OttomanSuleiman` folder to your CK3 mod directory:
   - Windows: `%USERPROFILE%\\Documents\\Paradox Interactive\\Crusader Kings III\\mod`
   - Linux: `~/.local/share/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings III/mod`
   - macOS: `~/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings III/mod`
2. Enable **Ottoman Empire: Suleiman I** in the Paradox launcher playset.
3. Start CK3 and choose **Suleiman the Magnificent (1546)** from the bookmark selector.

## Notes

This is a focused historical sandbox rather than a full conversion: it deliberately defines Suleiman's imperial title and core kingdoms while leaving the rest of the vanilla world available for emergent play. The mod targets CK3 `1.17.*`; update `supported_version` in both descriptor files if your installed game uses a different version.
