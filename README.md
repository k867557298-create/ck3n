# Ottoman Empire: Suleiman I

A **Crusader Kings III** mod that adds a compact, playable Ottoman scenario at the height of Sultan Suleiman I's rule.

## Included

- The **Suleiman the Magnificent** bookmark on **1 January 1546**, with a 1546 political map for the Ottoman Empire, Holy Roman Empire, France, England, and Russia.
- Sultan Suleiman I, Hürrem Sultan, Princes Mustafa, Selim, Bayezid, Cihangir, the late Prince Mehmed, Mihrimah Sultan, Grand Vizier Rüstem Pasha, and admiral Hayreddin Barbarossa.
- Contemporary rulers Charles V and Philip, Francis I and Henry II, Henry VIII and Edward VI, and Ivan IV—each connected to an immediate historical heir where applicable.
- A green-and-gold **Ottoman Empire** title headquartered in Constantinople, with Anatolia, Thrace, Armenia, Mesopotamia, Jerusalem, and Egypt recorded as its historic core kingdoms.
- Three Ottoman historical moments: the opening imperial briefing, the 1546 aftermath of Barbarossa's death, and the 1553 Prince Mustafa succession crisis. The Mustafa event lets the player choose an alternate history rather than forcing the historical outcome.
- English and Ukrainian localization.
- A decision for Turkish Ash'ari rulers who control Anatolia and Thrace to establish the Ottoman Empire in another campaign.

## Install

1. Copy both `descriptor.mod` and the `OttomanSuleiman` folder to your CK3 mod directory:
   - Windows: `%USERPROFILE%\\Documents\\Paradox Interactive\\Crusader Kings III\\mod`
   - Linux: `~/.local/share/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings III/mod`
   - macOS: `~/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings III/mod`
2. Enable **Ottoman Empire: Suleiman I** in the Paradox launcher playset.
3. Start CK3 and choose **Suleiman the Magnificent (1546)** from the bookmark selector.

## Notes

This is a focused historical sandbox rather than a full conversion: it deliberately defines the Ottoman court and the principal rulers required for the 1546 scenario while leaving the rest of the vanilla world available for emergent play. The mod targets CK3 `1.17.*`; update `supported_version` in both descriptor files if your installed game uses a different version.
